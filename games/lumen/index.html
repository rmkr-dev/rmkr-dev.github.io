Lumen

Lasers burn across a dark lab. Drag mirrors and splitters into their path and turn them until every target glows with exactly the colour it asks for.

How to play
  • Drag a gold-framed piece to any empty floor tile to move it. Drop it on another movable piece to swap them. Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn.
  • Mirrors bounce a beam 90°. Splitters (dashed glass) bounce half the beam and let the other half through.
  • Pivots sit on a round base: they turn but can't be moved. Grey pieces are bolted down.
  • Targets show the colour they need around their ring. Red and blue make magenta, red and green make yellow, green and blue make cyan, and all three make white. A target fed the wrong mix stays dark.
  • Walls stop beams. Glass lets beams through but nothing can stand on it. A coloured gate only lets its own colour pass.
  • Every move counts: a drag, a swap or a turn. Solve in par moves for ★★★, within par + 3 for ★★, any solve earns ★.
  • Undo takes back a move and Restart resets the level. Levels open one after another; your stars stay in this browser only.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter or Space picks up and drops a piece, X turns it, U or Backspace undoes, R restarts, P pauses, L opens levels, N goes to the next level, Esc cancels.
