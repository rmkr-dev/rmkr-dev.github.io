Number Forge

Combine number tiles two at a time to make the target. Start with plus and minus on three tiles; times and divide unlock as you climb.

How to play
  • Tap a number, tap a sign, then tap a second number. The two merge into their result.
  • Make any tile equal the target to clear the puzzle. You don't have to use every tile.
  • Results must be whole numbers above zero, so subtract the smaller number and only divide when it goes in evenly.
  • Journey: 15 puzzles over 5 tiers. Tiles go from 3 to 5, and × then ÷ unlock. You get 3 skips.
  • Blitz: 2 minutes. Each solve adds 5 seconds and a skip costs 10. Puzzles get harder every 3 solves.
  • Scoring: higher tiers are worth more. Finish at or under par (the fewest steps possible) for a bonus. In Journey, speed adds a bonus too. Clean solves in a row add up to +50%. A hint halves that puzzle's points.
  • Undo takes back one step, Reset goes back to the start of the puzzle, and Hint shows a step that works from where you are.
  • Keyboard: 1–5 pick tiles, + − * / pick the sign, Backspace or U undo, R reset, H hint, S skip, Esc clears, P pauses.
  • Each mode's best score stays in this browser only.
