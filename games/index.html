Focus Games

Short, simple brain games that stay in your browser. Large tap targets, offline after first load, and best scores saved only in localStorage. No sound effects in this wave.

Memory Match

Flip cards on a 4×4 board and find matching pairs. Beat your best move count.

Sequence Recall

Watch the pads light up, then tap the same pattern back. Climb as far as you can.

Number Slide

Classic 15-puzzle on a 4×4 grid. Slide tiles into order with as few moves as possible.

Word Scramble

Unscramble a common English word, then hit Next for another. Keep a streak going.

How it works

  • Everything runs client-side — nothing is uploaded.
  • Best score / streak values use localStorage keys prefixed with rmkr-game-.
  • No ads, accounts, or external game CDNs.
